What can I do?

TradeQuery answers questions about your MT5 trades. Every number it shows is checked against your own data.

Ask in your own words"How did I do last week?", "Which hour loses me money?", "Show my biggest losses". Follow-up questions remember what you were looking at.
Get a deeper answerTurn on "Deeper answer" under the question box to also get why it happened and what to check next. It takes a little longer.
Look at a day or a tradeTap a day on the calendar for its trades, then tap a trade number to see it on the price chart.
Understand the numbersNumbers on the home page have "What does this mean?" next to them. Your worst drop has its own step-by-step page.
Keep your data freshTap your account at the top right, then "Get new trades", or add an MT5 report file. You can also drop the file anywhere on the page.
Baskets (martingale and grid)Trades opened as one group count as a basket, so you can ask how deep a basket went.
Ready-made questions (instant, no AI needed)
Drop your MT5 report to add it
